Convene the 5-Advisor Council on: **$ARGUMENTS** (or, if empty, the change or question in front of
us). Read what's needed to judge it first (targeted reads), then answer as five independent advisors:

- **[Contrarian]:** 3–4 sentences on fatal risks: security, data loss, breaking changes, failure points.
- **[First Principles]:** 3–4 sentences questioning whether the premise is right.
- **[Expansionist]:** 3–4 sentences on scale, long-term architecture and unseen upside.
- **[Outsider]:** 3–4 sentences on clarity, maintainability and friction for the next person.
- **[Executor]:** the concrete next step and the smallest version that works.
- **[Chairman Synthesis]:** the verdict, the single biggest risk, and the change to make now.

Keep each block tight. Weigh UniVerse's limits (Cloudflare Workers Free and D1, no paid services
without the owner's yes, the safety rules in `AGENTS.md`). End with one plain-words
line for the owner.
