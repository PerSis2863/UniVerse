# UniVerse Impact - Global Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: November 19, 2026**

Universe Impact, located at Rue de la Patouillerie, 44700 Orvault (Nantes), France ("UniVerse Impact," "we," "our," or "us"), is deeply committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal data globally. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, sharing, and safeguarding of personal information processed through the UniVerse Impact platform at universeimpact.com (the "Platform"). The Platform is one account for several apps: **UniVersity** (schools and universities, including its Games section), **UniVerse Trace** (product passports), **UniVerse Relief** (help in emergencies), **UniVerse Green** (climate action, including Green Business) and **UniVerse Travel** (trips and travel guides). Section 2.8 describes the data each app adds.

This policy has been designed to comply with global privacy frameworks, including but not limited to:

- The European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR.
- The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), USA.
- The Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), India.
- The Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD), Brazil.

## 2. Categories of Personal Data We Collect

We collect and process the following categories of personal data globally:

### 2.2 Academic and Institutional Data

- Grades, quiz submissions, and calculated scores.
- Timetable scheduling, elective preferences, and major change requests.
- Uploaded academic documents, certificates, and assignments.
- **Campus network:** if your Institution is part of a network of campuses on UniVerse, the campus you belong to (worked out from your email address's domain, or set by an administrator), whether you let people at partner campuses find you in search (off unless you turn it on in Settings → Privacy), and, if you go on an exchange, the host campus and dates.

Certain data is considered "special category" (GDPR) or "sensitive personal data" (DPDPA/CCPA) and requires heightened protection:

- **Consent Management:** Digitally signed consents regarding institutional activities or data processing.
- **Safety Reports (BeeSafe):** Contextual descriptions of incidents reported via the BeeSafe campus security module.

### 2.5 Financial and Billing Data

- Records of payments generated for tuition, exams, library, and activity fees (including amount, type, and transaction status).
- Invoice history and generated receipts.

*Note:* UniVerse Impact does not collect, process, or store full credit card numbers. All payment processing is handled exclusively by our PCI-DSS compliant partners: Stripe, and Razorpay where a school in India uses it. This also covers brands' and companies' plans and campaigns in Trace and Green Business.

### 2.6 Communications and Interaction Data

- Contents of direct messages sent in private conversations and group chats, including photos, files, and voice notes.
- Call history (who called whom, when, and for how long). Calls are not recorded, except class calls that the class's teacher chooses to record (everyone in the call sees a REC badge); those recordings are added to the course's materials.
- Class notes: when a teacher turns on "Class notes" in a class call (everyone in the call sees a Notes badge and a notice), each participant's browser turns their speech into text and the teacher's browser sends that text transcript to us when the class ends. Audio is never sent or stored. The transcript is used once to make a study pack for the course (summary, notes, key moments, flashcards and a quiz the teacher reviews), which enrolled students can see. The transcript is deleted after 90 days; the study pack stays with the course until the teacher deletes it.
- Whiteboards you create or edit, including drawings and photos added to them.
- Public or semi-public group posts, knowledge hub articles, and announcements.
- Notes, feedback, and scheduling data from mentorship sessions and collaborative projects.
- Content you enter into AI features, such as questions to the study assistant and documents you ask us to summarize.
- **Voice typing and live captions:** these use your browser's own speech recognition. We receive only the resulting text, never the audio; some browsers (for example Google Chrome) send the audio to their maker's servers to turn it into text, under that company's privacy policy.
- **Connected accounts (optional):** if you connect Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Google Classroom or a Google or Outlook calendar, we keep the access keys that service gives us (encrypted, never sent to your browser) and the name of the connected account. What the connection shows (your newest mail, Slack direct messages, calendar events, Classroom classes) is read live from that service each time and is not stored by us, except classes and work you choose to import. You can disconnect at any time in Settings → Connected accounts; the keys are deleted.

- Push notification subscription tokens.

### 2.8 Data in UniVerse's Apps

Each app keeps only what it needs, and only when you use it:

- **UniVerse Trace.** When you scan a product's code: the code, the time, the kind of device (Android, iPhone, Windows or Mac), an approximate location (the city) worked out from your connection, and, only if you allow it in your browser, your device's location, used to tell a genuine product from a likely copy. Maps of scans never show a place more precisely than about 10 km. If you choose: points in your wallet, warranties, ownership records, reviews, repairs, take-back drop-offs, and reports of fake labels. For people working for a brand, warehouse, carrier or shop: the stops they record (place and time) and their team membership. For brands: business details, products, batches, campaigns and payments.

## 4. How We Use Your Data

We process your data strictly to:

1. Provide, maintain, and continuously improve the Platform and its modules.
2. Authenticate your identity and maintain rigorous security perimeters.
3. Enable academic workflows, including grading, attendance, and timetabling for your Institution.
4. Power the AI Skill-to-Project Matching Engine, utilizing your skills and academic history to surface highly relevant NGO projects and internships, and answer requests you make to AI features (see section 5.3).
5. Issue, cryptographically hash, and verify blockchain-anchored Impact Credentials.
6. Facilitate payment processing and generate associated billing receipts via Stripe.
7. Route critical safety alerts (BeeSafe) directly to authorized campus administrators.
8. Deliver in-app notifications, announcements, and strictly necessary administrative emails.
9. Generate anonymized and aggregated analytics for institutional reporting and platform research.
10. Detect abuse, mitigate fraud, and investigate security threats via comprehensive audit logging.
11. **Early warning for students who may be struggling.** Once a day, simple published rules look at attendance, grades, quizzes not handed in and how recently you used the Platform in each course, and list students who may benefit from support, together with the reasons. The list is visible only to the teacher of the course and to your Institution's administrators. It is not an automated decision: no grade, status or access changes because of it, and a person decides whether to contact you. It uses no AI and no special-category data. The legal basis is your Institution's legitimate interest (or public task) in supporting student success; you may object at any time by contacting your Institution or us, and you can request to see any entry about you (it is included in "Download my data").
12. **Translation of chat messages** when you or another member of a chat asks for it (see section 5.3).
13. **Alerts and reminders:** nearby requests for help and emergencies (Relief), travel-document and trip reminders and a morning briefing (Travel), and reminders to come back to your courses. You choose most of these in Settings, and emergency notices go only to the people they concern.
14. **Spotting likely copies (Trace):** simple published rules flag a label scanned far from its first scan or too often. This says something about a product's label, not about you, and changes nothing in your account.

We unequivocally do not sell your personal data to third parties, nor do we utilize your personal data for third-party cross-context behavioral advertising (as defined under CCPA).

## 6. Blockchain and Impact Credentials

When an Impact Credential is successfully issued to you upon project completion, the Platform generates a SHA-256 cryptographic hash of the core credential payload (comprising your name, project title, issuance date, and institution). This hash is formatted as a W3C Verifiable Credential v1.1 and may be anchored on the Polygon public blockchain. Only the hash is recorded there; it cannot be turned back into your name or other details.

- **Public Verification:** The public verification endpoint (e.g., `/verify/[id]`) enables third parties to verify the authenticity of your credential. If you do not wish for your credential to be publicly verifiable, do not distribute the verification link.
- **Immutability and Erasure:** Blockchain cryptographic hashes are, by definition, immutable records. Deleting a hash from the underlying audit chain would permanently compromise the mathematical integrity of all subsequent verifications. While the hash itself cannot be "deleted," upon receiving a valid request for erasure (under GDPR, CCPA, or DPDPA), we will immediately suppress the public display and accessibility of your credential through our interfaces.

- **Skills Passport and Open Badges:** You can choose to publish a Skills Passport page, which shows only the sections you select (verified credentials, impact totals, skills, course names — never your email, phone, grades or date of birth). It is private until you publish it, you can make it private again or replace its link at any time, and search engines are asked not to index it. You can also download your credentials as Open Badges 3.0 files; these contain your name and a salted hash of your email address (not the address itself).

## 9. Your Global Data Protection Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the EU, UK, USA/California, India, Brazil, etc.), you possess specific rights regarding your personal data:

- **Right of Access / Right to Know:** Request a comprehensive copy of the personal data we hold about you and details on how it is processed.
- **Right to Rectification / Correction:** Request correction of inaccurate, obsolete, or incomplete data.
- **Right to Erasure / Deletion ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Request the deletion of your personal data where no overriding legal obligation requires its retention. You can ask directly in the app (Settings → Privacy → Delete my account). To protect you against someone else deleting your account from an unlocked device, each request is checked before the account is erased, normally within a few days and always within one month; we email you when it is done. Records that others legitimately need (for example a class register) are kept with your name and contact details removed.
- **Right to Restrict Processing:** Request a temporary halt to the processing of your data under specific conditions.
- **Right to Data Portability:** Receive a copy of your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format (e.g., JSON export).
- **Instructions After Death (France):** Give instructions on what should happen to your personal data after your death.
- **Right to Object / Opt-Out:** Object to processing based on legitimate interests, including the generation of AI-based recommendations, and opt-out of any marketing communications. (Under CCPA, you have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data, though we do not engage in such practices).
- **Right to Non-Discrimination:** You will not receive discriminatory treatment for exercising your privacy rights.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Revoke consent previously granted for medical data processing or marketing communications.
- **Right to Lodge a Complaint:** File a formal complaint with a Data Protection Authority: our lead authority, the CNIL (France, www.cnil.fr), or the authority where you live (e.g., ICO, California Privacy Protection Agency, Data Protection Board of India).

To exercise any of these rights, please submit a written request to privacy@universeimpact.com. We will process and respond to your request within the timeframe mandated by your local law (typically 30-45 days).

## 10. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Platform relies minimally on client-side storage to function securely:

- **Optional page counts:** only if you choose "Accept all", we count which pages are visited with PostHog (hosted in the EU), using a random number kept on your device, never your name, with any identifiers or links in the address blanked. It never runs when your browser sends Global Privacy Control, and you can change your choice at any time at universeimpact.com/privacy-choices.
- **Essential Cookies:** Required for session management, Firebase authentication token handling, and Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) protection. These cannot be disabled.
- **Local Storage:** Utilized to cache authentication tokens, user interface preferences (e.g., dark mode), and temporary state to improve performance.
- **Push Tokens:** Stored only if you explicitly grant the browser permission to receive push notifications.

We do not use advertising cookies, advertising pixels or social media trackers. Because the page counts are optional, we ask first in a cookie notice. See also universeimpact.com/privacy-choices.

## 11. Data Security

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures globally to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

- **Encryption:** HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit; encryption at rest for the database (Cloudflare D1) and stored files (Cloudflare R2).
- **Authentication:** Industry-standard OAuth 2.0 and JWT-based identity management via Firebase.
- **Access Control:** Strict Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC) ensuring data is only visible to appropriately credentialed roles, checked on every request.
- **Location:** places are rounded before they are shown on any map (about 500 m for Relief, about 10 km for Green and Trace), so no person or home can be found from them.
- **Connected accounts:** the keys from services you connect are encrypted and never sent to a browser.
- **Breach Notification:** In the event of a personal data breach posing a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the competent supervisory authorities globally in accordance with statutory timelines (e.g., within 72 hours under GDPR, as promptly as possible under DPDPA/CCPA).
